Privacy Policy — Plate

Last updated: November 28, 2025

Plate (“the app”) is a nutrition tracker published as com.stockeasy.plate. Nutrition logs, goals, profile, rank/XP data, and your optional API key are stored only on your device (browser local storage inside the app). We do not operate a user account or cloud database for Plate.

Information you provide

  • Profile fields (age, height, weight, goals, city, diet preference) — on-device only.
  • Food logs and photos you choose to analyze — on-device only, except when you use AI features below.
  • Groq API key (optional) — stored on-device; used only when you run photo or text analysis or meal suggestions.

Third-party services

If you add a Groq API key, the app sends food photos, descriptions, or meal-suggestion prompts to api.groq.com using your key. Groq’s terms and privacy policy apply to that traffic. We do not receive or store your key on our servers.

Permissions

The Android app uses Internet access for Groq when you configure a key. Food photos are selected through the system file picker; we do not require location permissions.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Do not use it for medical treatment decisions without professional advice.

Contact

Questions: use the support email listed on your Google Play store listing.

Changes

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date will change. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.

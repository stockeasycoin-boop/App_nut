Plate is a beautiful, simple nutrition tracker for Android.

• Log food with a photo, a short description, or manual entry
• Daily calorie ring and macro rings (protein, carbs, fat, fiber)
• Sugar, sodium, and key micronutrient targets you can edit
• Rank, XP, and streak rewards when you stay on track
• AI meal ideas and local protein tips (optional Groq API key — free tier)
• Everything stays on your device — no account required

Set your profile once. Targets use standard formulas (you can tweak them in Settings). Snap a plate, adjust the portion slider, and save.

Groq powers optional photo and text analysis and meal suggestions. Each user adds their own free API key in Settings; keys are never sent to us.

Plate is for personal tracking, not medical advice. Talk to a clinician before following an aggressive calorie deficit.
